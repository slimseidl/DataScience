Types of Studies 

    - Experiments 

        - an experiment is defined by comparing outcomes between two or more groups and ensuring equivalence between the compared groups except for the manipulation that we want to test
        - see if a change in one feature has an effect in the value of a second feature
            -  seeing if changing the layout of a button on a website causes more visitors to click on it

    - Observational Studies

        - no control is exerted on the variables of interest, perhaps due to ethical concerns or a lack of power
        - look at the effects of smoking on health, the potential risks make it unethical to force people into smoking

    - Quasi-Experiments

        - some, but not all, of the control requirements of a true experiment, are met
        - rolling out a new website interface to all users to see how much time they spend on it
        - manipulation is controlled by the experimenter, there aren't multiple groups to compare
