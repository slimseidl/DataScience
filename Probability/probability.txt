Binomial Distribution

    - Determines the probability of a string of independent 'coin flip like events'.

    - With an odd number of coin flips, you will always have either more Heads or more Tails.
    
    Five Flips, Two Heads 

        - Place H in first slot of truth table 
        - There are 4 remaining spots heads could come up 
            - but double counted because of outcomes so have to divide by 2
        Oucomes = 5 * 4 / 2 = 10 
    
    Binomial Distribution Formula:
    
        10 Flips 4 Heads 

            10 * 9 * 8 * 7 / (4 * 3 * 2 * 1) = 210
            Number of scenarios * factorial of number of probabilities divided by probabilities factored 
        
        Example: 
            10 Flips 5 Heads 
        - Calculate number of available positions
            - 5 positions (heads)
                - 10 * 9 *8 *7*6 = 30240
        - Calc Overcounting 
            - 5 * 4 *3 * 2 * 1 = 120 
        - Divide 
            - 30240 / 120 = 252 

        5 Flips 1 head 

        Probability = n! / k!(n-K)^! / Size of truth table 

        Truth table size = number of possible outcomes to the power of number of flips 

            2 ^ 5 = 32 

            5/32 = .15



 Conditional Probability 

    - The probability of the outcome of one event is affected by the outcome of another event.  

    Cancer Example 

        - P(c) of having cancer = 0.1 
        - P(p) positive test = 0.9 
        - P(n) negative test = 0.1

        - Whats the probability of a positive test regardless if have cancer or not? 

            Take .1 * .9 = .09 + (1-.9) * (1-.8)
                = .27 
        

        P(Pos) = P(c) * P(p|c) + P(-c) + P(pos|-c)


    - Take the probability of first condition timse probability of second condition to get overall Conditional probability


    - For probability of positive test regardless of status, add up positive test probabilities
