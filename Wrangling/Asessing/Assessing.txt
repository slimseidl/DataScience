Dirty Data vs Messy Data 

    - Dirty = inaccurate, or a data quality issue 
    - Messy = poor structure, unorganized

Data Quality Metrics 

    - Completeness 
        - a metric that helps you understand whether your data is sufficient to answer interesting questions or solve your problem.
        - missing data in incomplete / NAN 
        - Can remove
        - can replace / impute the means 
    - Validity
        - helping you understand how well your data conforms to a defined set of rules for data, also known as a schema
        - Zip codes should be integer 
        - does data lie within a range? 
    - Accuracy 
        -  helps you understand whether your data accurately represents the reality it aims to depict
        - uncalibrated scales record 5lbs heavier 
    - Consistency 
        - helps you understand two things:
            - whether your data follows a standard format 
            - whether your data’s info matches with information from other data sources
        - Mm/dd/yy format vs. dd/mm/yy 
        - needs to have same formats 
    - Uniqueness
        - helps you understand whether there are duplicate or overlapping values in your data
